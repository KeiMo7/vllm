# Fair-Share Scheduling — Acceptance Report

Date: 2026-11-23. Change: new `fairshare` scheduling policy for the vLLM V1
scheduler (per-tenant weighted fair shares, reservations, preemption
accounting, windowed settlement, fairness reports). FCFS/priority paths are
unchanged when the policy is off.

## Acceptance criteria — measured results

### 1. Share deviation under burst traffic (threshold 5%)

Two tenants, weights `A=2.0, B=1.0`, both backlogged for the whole run
(100 requests each, 60 scheduling steps, `max_num_seqs=4`):

| tenant | tokens charged | share  | expected | deviation |
|--------|----------------|--------|----------|-----------|
| A      | 448            | 0.6667 | 0.6667   | +0.0000   |
| B      | 224            | 0.3333 | 0.3333   | −0.0000   |

Source: `measurements.json → share_deviation`, `fairness_report.json`.
Test: `test_burst_traffic_share_deviation`.

### 2. Low-weight tenant worst wait (bound: 48 rounds)

Weights `H=4.0, L=1.0`, H bursting (100 requests), 8 low-weight requests
injected one at a time:

- worst wait measured: **25 scheduling rounds** (bound 48 = two batch
  turnovers), 0.0085 s wall;
- all 8 low-weight requests completed — no starvation.

Source: `measurements.json → low_weight_wait`.
Test: `test_low_weight_tenant_max_wait_bounded`.

### 3. Replay determinism

Three tenants, mixed priorities, fixed arrival sequence, 50 steps, two
independent runs: scheduled request-id sequence **identical**
(`measurements.json → replay_determinism.identical = true`).
Test: `test_deterministic_replay`.

### 4. Single-tenant throughput vs. baseline

Same 60-request workload, `fcfs` vs. `fairshare` with one tenant:

| policy    | steps to drain | scheduled tokens | schedule sequence |
|-----------|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| fcfs      | 75             | 300              | (baseline)        |
| fairshare | 75             | 300              | identical to fcfs |

Throughput regression: **0** (schedule sequences identical). Scheduling
wall-clock overhead across repeated runs: 0.93×–1.44× of baseline on this
CPU microbenchmark (millisecond scale, dominated by noise; asserted < 2×).
Source: `measurements.json → single_tenant_throughput`.
Test: `test_single_tenant_throughput_matches_baseline`.

## Files in this directory

- `fairness_report.json` — per-tenant fairness report (expected vs. actual
  share, deviation, wait distribution, preemptions) for the burst scenario.
- `fair_share_windows.jsonl` — per-window settlement detail (one JSON
  object per accounting window; usage never carries across windows).
- `measurements.json` — the raw numbers above, regenerated by
  `generate_measurements.py`.
- `test_output_fair_share.txt` — pytest output of the new suite.
- `test_output_existing_scheduler.txt` — pytest summaries of the existing
  scheduler suites.
- `generate_measurements.py` — reproduces `measurements.json`
  (run from repo root: `.venv/bin/python reports/generate_measurements.py`).
